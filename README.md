## 💡 Why Japan Tech?

Japan's tech industry combines **world-class engineering discipline** with a culture of deep technical investment. What you'll find in these blogs:

- Cutting-edge **LLM production systems** from companies running at massive scale
- Deep dives into **distributed systems** powering hundreds of millions of users
- Pioneering work in **recommendation engines**, NLP for Japanese, and computer vision
- Rare access to **inside architecture decisions** at companies like Mercari, Line, and Rakuten

Whether you're an AI engineer, backend architect, or just curious — this list has you covered.
